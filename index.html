    Questions, answered

    Is Verbi free?

    Yes, Verbi is free to download. The free plan includes 60 minutes of transcription a month, live transcription (basic on iPhone), one automatic AI summary and copying the text, and keeps recordings for 15 days. Verbi Pro is an optional subscription without those limits; the app shows its price in your currency before you subscribe.

    Which languages does Verbi understand?

    Verbi transcribes 39 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Turkish, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Japanese and Chinese. You can pick the language or leave it on auto-detect.

    Can Verbi transcribe Turkish and German?

    Yes. Both are among the 39 languages Verbi transcribes, and the app itself is available in Turkish and German too, as well as English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian (Portuguese and Italian from version 1.4.0 on iPhone and 1.2.0 on Android).

    How long can a recording be?

    There’s no length limit with Verbi Pro: an hour-long meeting or a whole lecture is transcribed from start to finish. On the free plan, your recordings share 60 minutes a month, and on iPhone a recording stops when they run out. On Android, and on iPhone with iOS 26 or later, you can leave the app while a long recording transcribes and Verbi tells you when it’s ready; on older iOS versions, transcription continues when you reopen the app.

    Where are my recordings stored?

    On your phone. Verbi has no account and no cloud sync, so recordings and transcripts are stored on your device. To transcribe, the audio is sent to our speech-to-text providers (ElevenLabs and OpenAI), usually through our server; summaries, chat and translation send the transcript text to OpenAI. The privacy policy explains what is sent, where, and for how long.

    Does Verbi summarize recordings?

    Yes. With Pro, choose a template (general meeting, 1:1 meeting, daily or weekly meeting, lesson notes, idea capture, action items, email draft or blog post) and the summary comes back in that shape; Pro also summarizes every transcript on its own. Summaries stick to what was said: owners and due dates appear only when someone stated them. With Pro you can also ask the transcript questions. On the free plan, your first transcript gets one summary automatically, with your default template.

    Is Verbi available on Android?

    Yes. Verbi is on Google Play for Android phones and on the App Store for iPhone.

    Can I transcribe audio files and videos I already have?

    Yes. Import an audio file, share audio to Verbi from another app, or paste a YouTube link, and Verbi transcribes it like a recording made in the app. Imported files count toward the free plan’s 60 minutes a month, and the app shows any free-plan limit on video imports.
